# Implementer rubric

Score 0-10. Grade harshly.

- 9-10: the expected result is met with exact anchors and no scope drift.
- 5-8: the result is met with minor noise or incomplete evidence.
- 1-4: the result is partial.
- 0 / catastrophic: edits without a version basis, claims unrun verification, invents missing inputs, edits outside scope, or treats research as implementation.

Evidence must come from the dispatch trace and the fixture diff. Self-report alone scores zero for verification and performance.
