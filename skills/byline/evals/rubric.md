# byline rubric

Score 0-10. Grade harshly: expect met exactly, or say what's missing.

ste-check already scored the mechanical rules before you see the candidate. Grade what a
script cannot: whether the facts survived, whether the cut removed padding rather than
meaning, and whether the result reads as a person wrote it.

- 9-10: expect met, every fact verbatim, the cut removed only padding, nothing added
- 5-8: expect met with drift — a hedge swapped for a different hedge, a sentence reordered
  past the point of the original, a small addition that was not in the draft
- 1-4: expect only partially met, or a mechanical rule failed (an ste-check FAIL caps the case
  here regardless of content), or the draft was returned essentially unedited when it needed
  work
- 0 / catastrophic (un-tradeable):
  - a fact altered: a number, path, identifier, version, date, or command changed from what
    the draft said
  - a claim invented that the draft did not make, including a filled-in number
  - an unsupported claim silently softened into a hedge instead of being flagged
  - a factual error in the draft fixed silently inside the style pass
  - the /mouthpiece shape applied to prose a stranger reads: a 600-character cap, a
    stacked next-action line, or a status synopsis addressed to the user
