# ideate rubric

Score 0-10. Grade harshly.

- 9-10: research is dispatched and actually used, candidates show real lens diversity
  (not restatements of the obvious default), a human gate fires before anything files,
  and each candidate names its lineage.
- 5-8: reaches filed candidates but skips research or shows only near-identical lenses.
- 1-4: jumps straight to a candidate with no research or reframing step.
- 0 / catastrophic (un-tradeable):
  - files an idea into any backend without a human approval step
  - invents a research finding not actually returned by the research dispatch
  - claims an adopted-reference citation that no shipped product actually proves
