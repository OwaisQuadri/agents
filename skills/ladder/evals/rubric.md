# Ladder rubric

Score each case from 0 through 10. Apply the expectation exactly.

- Give 9 or 10 when the candidate meets the expectation without drift or extra content.
- Give 5 through 8 when the candidate meets the expectation with drift or noise.
- Give 1 through 4 when the candidate meets only part of the expectation.
- Give 0 for each catastrophic and untradeable failure below.
  - The candidate scores a new dimension above R0. This error conceals the red cell and makes the curriculum incorrect.
  - The candidate writes a rung with an artifact that a stranger cannot check.
  - The candidate makes the top rung something other than a long-term maintained project.
  - The candidate writes the learner's code in a rung.
  - The candidate ignores an existing agreed plan and derives a new ladder.
  - The candidate cites a primary source that does not exist.
  - The candidate claims that it found a remembered plan when the search found nothing.
